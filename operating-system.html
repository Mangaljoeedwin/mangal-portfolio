THE PMM CLARITY FRAMEWORK

Product marketing as a connected system.

A practical operating model that connects what the market teaches us to what the business does next.

THE CORE IDEA

Clarity is not a messaging deliverable. It is the outcome of making a sequence of good decisions, then learning from what happens in the market.
